# Goldenrod Park
Goldenrod Park is a passion project of mine that I have been working on throughout the past year on and off  
My goal is to create a cozy social game for those who want a low maintenance way to play and chat with friends with no microtransactions  
While I have no pictures to share of the game because I am still working on systems I can share a brief summary.  

Goldenrod park is a cozy social game designed in a similar low res retro style to the game webfishing  
As a player there are many things to do such as various minigames, fishing, card collecting, and house decorating  
There will be many ways to earn cosmetics and customize both your character and housing to personalize your experience
