# Destiny 2 Armor Website/App

My love for Destiny as a franchise cannot be understated. It is part of who I am today and I wouldn't be the same person had I not found it in 2019.  
With the end of service announcement Bungie released the best free update the game has ever receieved, including a complete reward overhaul.  
They introduced hundered of new and reprised items which gives you a lot to look for a grind for. However, the D2 sphere lacked a good website/app for planning armor sets out.  
My goal with this website/app is to create something that can be used in conjunction with other established apps and websites to make a todo list.
